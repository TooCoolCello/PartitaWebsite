How they're built

One job each

Each app does one thing well. Install the one you need; none of them depends on another.

No account

Nothing to sign in to and no vendor suite running in the background. Your settings stay on your PC.

Out of the way

They live in the tray and can start with Windows. Open one when you need it and forget it when you don't.

Why the names?

Each app is named after a marking you'd find in a cello part.

Partita
A suite of pieces for one instrument, like Bach's partitas for solo violin. Here, a suite of apps.
Divisi
Written when a section splits, some players taking the top line and some the bottom. Divisi splits an app's sound across your outputs.
Tacet
Tells a player to stay silent for the whole passage. In Tacet, it's the noise that stays silent.
Giusto
As in tempo giusto: exactly the right tempo. Giusto will make your headphones sound exactly right.
