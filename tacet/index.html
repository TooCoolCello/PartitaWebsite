Questions

Does it work with Discord, OBS and games?

Yes. Anything that lets you pick a microphone can use Tacet: choose CABLE Output.

Why do I need VB-Cable?

Apps can only listen to audio devices, so Tacet needs a virtual one to send your voice to. VB-Cable is free, but its license doesn't allow bundling it.

Will it cut me off when I sing?

No. The gate tells singing from steady noise, so held notes come through.

Can I use speakers instead of headphones?

Yes. Echo cancellation takes what your speakers play out of your mic.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No account. It only goes online to check GitHub for updates once a day, and you can turn that off.

What if an update goes wrong?

If an update can't be installed, the version you had keeps running. Your settings are backed up too, the last 10 versions.
